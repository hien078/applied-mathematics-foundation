First-principles mathematical foundations for machine learning and artificial intelligence. Rigorous derivations, geometric intuition, interactive Jupyter notebooks, and NumPy-verified implementations synchronized from [first-principles-math-modeling](https://github.com/hien078/first-principles-math-modeling).

> 📌 **Sister Repository:** Applied algorithm implementations and full ML model pipelines are available in [Machine-Learning-from-scratch](https://github.com/hien078/Machine-Learning-from-scratch).

## 🏛️ Comprehensive Mathematical Curriculum

### 1. 📐 Linear Algebra ([`linear_algebra/`](linear_algebra/README.md))
- **01.** Vector Spaces & Subspaces
- **02.** Linear Maps & Matrix Transformations
- **03.** Linear Systems & Direct Factorizations (LU, Cholesky)
- **04.** Orthogonality, Projections & QR Decomposition
- **05.** Determinants, Trace & Matrix Polynomials
- **06.** Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors & Spectral Theory
- **07.** Canonical Forms & Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)
- **08.** Numerical Linear Algebra & Iterative Solvers (CG, GMRES)
- **09.** Numerical Spectrum Algorithms (QR Algorithm, Power Method)
- **10.** Matrix Calculus, Graph Theory & AI Applications

### 2. 📈 Single & Multivariable Calculus ([`calculus/`](calculus/README.md))
- **01.** Functions & Fundamental Properties
- **02.** Limits & Continuity
- **03.** Single-Variable Derivatives & Chain Rule
- **04.** Derivative Applications & Optimization
- **05.** Indefinite & Definite Integrals
- **06.** Integral Applications in Geometry & Physics
- **07.** Improper Integrals & Special Functions (Gamma, Beta)
- **08.** Sequences, Series & Convergence Tests
- **09.** Taylor & Power Series
- **10.** Multivariable Functions & Partial Derivatives
- **11.** Gradients & Directional Derivatives
- **12.** Hessian, Jacobian & Curvature
- **13.** Multiple Integrals & Coordinate Transformations
- **14.** Vector Calculus & Field Theorems (Green, Stokes, Divergence)
- **15.** Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs)

### 3. 🎯 Optimization & Calculus Optimization ([`optimization/`](optimization/README.md), [`calculus_optimization/`](calculus_optimization/README.md))
- Unconstrained & Constrained Optimization (Lagrange Multipliers, KKT Conditions)
- Gradient Descent Mechanics, Line Search, Newton-Raphson & Quasi-Newton (BFGS)
- Optimization Landscapes, Smoothness, Convexity & Taylor Approximations

### 4. 🎲 Probability & Statistics ([`probability_statistics/`](probability_statistics/README.md))
- Probability Basics, Axioms & Conditional Probability
- Discrete & Continuous Distributions, Multivariate Normal
- Expectation, Variance, Covariance & Law of Large Numbers
- Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) & Bayesian Inference

### 5. 📊 Information Theory ([`information_theory/`](information_theory/README.md))
- Information Entropy, Joint & Conditional Entropy
- Cross-Entropy, Relative Entropy & KL Divergence
- Mutual Information & Applications in Deep Learning / VAEs

### 6. ⚙️ Numerical Methods & Computing ([`numerical_methods/`](numerical_methods/README.md), [`numerical_computing/`](numerical_computing/README.md))
- Floating-Point Arithmetic, Overflow/Underflow & Stability
- Conditioning, Condition Numbers & Ill-Posed Problems
- Vectorization & High-Performance Matrix Operations in NumPy

### 7. 🕸️ Graph Theory & Mathematical Reasoning ([`graph_theory/`](graph_theory/README.md), [`mathematical_reasoning/`](mathematical_reasoning/README.md))
- Graph Representations, Adjacency Matrices & Spectral Graph Theory
- Mathematical Proof Techniques, Formal Induction & Symbolic Computation

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Clone the Repository
```bash
git clone https://github.com/hien078/Mathematics-for-ML-from-scratch.git
cd Mathematics-for-ML-from-scratch
```

### 2. Set Up Environment
```bash
python3 -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

### 3. Launch JupyterLab
```bash
jupyter lab
```
